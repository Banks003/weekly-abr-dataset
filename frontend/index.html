The Australian Business Register is the canonical record of every Australian Business Number issued by the Australian Taxation Office. This gazetteer republishes the weekly bulk extract in a form any modern browser can query directly — no installation, no account, no rate limit.

Searches run client-side. Data is fetched on demand from gazetteer.au; nothing about your queries is logged.

Source
